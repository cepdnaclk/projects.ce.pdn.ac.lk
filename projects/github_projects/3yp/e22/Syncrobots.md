Infrastructure-free formation control for low-cost robot swarms using Ultra-Wideband (UWB) ranging instead of cameras, markers, or GPS. Each follower tracks distances to both the global leader and its immediate predecessor, damping chain oscillations and enabling affordable multi-robot experimentation for student teams.
